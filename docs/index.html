( 04 — Privacy )

Offline isn't a feature.
It's the architecture.

Most apps promise not to misuse your data. My Skin Routine asks the system for no Internet permission at all — it cannot call home, even by accident.

AndroidManifest.xml
<manifest …>

  <uses-permission android:name=
      "android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS"/>
  <uses-permission android:name=
      "android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM"/>

  <!-- no android.permission.INTERNET —
       the app cannot reach the network -->

</manifest>
  • No Internet permission — your data physically cannot leave the device.
  • Local SQLite database — products, routines, journal: everything stays on your phone.
  • Zero third-party SDK — no analytics, no crash reporters, no ad networks.
  • Your backups, your rules — one-tap ZIP export of everything, restored anywhere.
( 05 — FAQ )

Questions, answered

Do I need an account?+
No. The app has no login, no servers and no sign-up flow. Install, open, start.
Where does my data go?+
Nowhere. Everything lives in a local SQLite database on your device. The only way data leaves your phone is if you export a ZIP backup yourself.
Is this a medical app?+
No. My Skin Routine is a wellness tracker. It does not diagnose, treat or advise on any condition — always see a dermatologist or doctor about medical concerns.
How much does it cost?+
Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no premium tier.
Which devices are supported?+
Android 5.0 and up. The interface is available in English and French.
Can I move my data to a new phone?+
Yes — Settings → Export creates a single ZIP with your database and photos; import it on the new device and everything is restored.
