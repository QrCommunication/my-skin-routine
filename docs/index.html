Privacy first

Your skin data
stays yours.

The app has no Internet permission at all. Technically unable to phone home — not just promised to.

  • ●No account, no sign-up — open the app and start.

  • ●ZIP backup export — your data and photos, in your hands.
  • ●Local database — everything stored in an on-device SQLite database.
